Financial Management & Investments

M&A

Merger, Acquisition & Selling Strategy

Support organizations in developing external growth strategies through mergers, acquisitions, joint ventures, strategic alliances, and partnerships. We also assist businesses in evaluating divestment, carve-out, and spin-off opportunities to refocus on their core activities.

Target Screening

Identify and assess potential acquisition targets and strategic partners according to defined criteria, including industry, company size, geographical location, business performance, and strategic fit.
